Our story

A Pickering family business, since 1946.

Albright Electric was established in 1946 and has been a family-owned and operated business for three generations. Albright isn't just our company name, it's our family name. Let our family look after your family.

We offer electrical services to residential, commercial and industrial clients in Pickering, Ajax, Durham Region, the GTA and Cottage Country. We believe in quality work and quality parts. All our work is 100% guaranteed.

Owner and operator Milton Albright started on the job site with his dad, completed his apprenticeship at George Brown College, and became a Master Electrician in 1986. The office is led by office manager Peggy Albright, who coordinates your job with our crew, your hydro utility and ESA inspectors.

  • Master electricians on the team
  • Electrical Safety Authority and Approved Contractor Program
  • Available 24/7 for electrical emergencies
